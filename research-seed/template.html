02 / DETECTION & ALERTS

Detect changes. Don't invent certainty.

What is built

A local CSV snapshot detector preserves evidence, compares player/game records and emits unconfirmed change candidates. Fractional sacks and missing values are handled explicitly.

03 / RESEARCH STANDARD

Evidence before conclusions.

Possible? Yes, with limits.

Published statistical changes can be detected automatically. A feed edit alone cannot prove an NFL correction or changed settlement. The database distinguishes actual record changes, apparent-final rulings and non-changes.

How often? Not established.

These selected cases do not supply a league-wide frequency. No season is fully audited. Extra points, two-point conversions, safeties and interceptions still need verified historical cases.

Who controls the record?

The NFL statistical guide, p.4 makes stadium judgments subject to NFL and official-statistician review. Fantasy platforms and sportsbooks have separate settlement rules.
