⚠️ Key Finding

NFL scoring records can and do change after games conclude. The Elias Sports Bureau issues corrections weekly, typically by Thursday, but corrections have arrived months after the original game. While actual game scores rarely change post-game, statistical corrections frequently cross market-relevant thresholds for fantasy football, player props, and season-long bets.

How the NFL Stat Correction Process Works

1

Game Day (Sunday/Monday/Thursday)

Live statisticians at the game enter every play into the NFL's GSIS system. Errors are common — wrong player number, incorrect yardage, misattributed plays. Most are corrected immediately, but some persist.

2

Overnight Load (Monday/Tuesday)

Fantasy platforms load "official" stats from Elias Sports Bureau or STATS LLC. This catches most remaining errors. Stats are now considered "semi-official."

3

Elias Review (Wednesday)

Elias Sports Bureau meets with the NFL to review all game tape and issue official statistic changes. Coaches may also appeal specific rulings.

4

Corrections Published (Thursday)

Official corrections distributed to fantasy platforms and published at NFL.com Stat Corrections. Platforms process corrections Thursday morning (4AM-4PM ET depending on platform).

⚡

Late Corrections (Anytime)

There is no hard deadline. Corrections have been issued weeks or months after the original game. Example: Caleb Williams' Week 6, 2024 correction arrived in Week 16 — 10 weeks later.
