## Separate downstream-effect report (not a full correction case)

The AP story is marked “FOR RELEASE” Dec. 31, 2011; ESPN page metadata says “Updated: Dec. 28” (pre-release). It reports that a Bills-defense extra sack against Washington changed Adam Gizelbach's fantasy matchup from a win by just over one point to a loss by less than one; he said the two-point change cost him money. It does not state the exact game week, original/corrected sack totals, or correction timestamp, and it documents no sportsbook. It is recorded in `data/reported-downstream-effects.json` as an attributed, source-limited fantasy outcome.

## Reconciliation and counts

- 9 game cases; 11 distinct player names; 15 player-stat deltas.
- All 9 cases have a date attached to a cited source/report, but those are not uniformly official correction timestamps. Seven cited dates are 2–4 days after the game, Caleb Williams is 65 days, and the AP Rodgers article is 76 days after the game; that retrospective article does not reveal the actual NFL correction date. The Spiller/Kamara table dates are not independently re-openable in this review.
- 0 of these 9 cases documents a changed NFL final score or winner.
- 0 of these 9 cases has a verified sportsbook line and settlement change.
- 1 separate AP report describes an actual fantasy matchup flip, but is not a sportsbook outcome and lacks enough fields for a full correction row.

The counts above are sample descriptions only. They are not correction frequencies, estimates of market exposure, or proof that no other outcomes occurred.
